Why Consider a HELOC

The first mortgage stays. The HELOC is separate

A home equity line of credit is a second loan secured by your home. Opening one leaves your existing first mortgage and its terms in place. The HELOC has its own payment, its own costs and its own repayment obligations, so your total monthly outgo changes even though the first mortgage does not.
