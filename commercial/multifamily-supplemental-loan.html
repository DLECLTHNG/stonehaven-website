A supplemental apartment loan or a full refinance?

A multifamily supplemental loan adds debt secured by an apartment property while the first mortgage stays in place. Compare the cash available after fees and the cost of both payments with the proceeds and payment on a full refinance.

Who can arrange a supplemental loan?

Start with the most recent mortgage statement and identify the owner of the first loan, its servicing company and the original closing date. Fannie Mae’s published supplemental program requires its own existing mortgage and requires the lender to be the current servicer. Freddie Mac’s rules depend in part on whether the first loan has been securitized. Ask the servicer to confirm who may originate the request. Fannie Mae supplemental terms · Freddie Mac supplemental terms (PDF).

A supplement generally adds a subordinate mortgage. The first mortgage stays in place, including its payment schedule and restrictions. Confirm the proposed use of funds, seasoning and whether the two loans would mature together. Stonehaven can help compare that route with a replacement first mortgage.

How much cash would each option actually release?

Illustrative comparison. Assume a proposed $5.5 million refinance, a $4.4 million first mortgage payoff before the prepayment charge, a $175,000 prepayment charge and $125,000 of costs and escrows. That leaves $800,000 at closing. If an eligible supplemental proposal provides $900,000 with $65,000 of costs and escrows, it would release $835,000 while leaving the first loan outstanding.

The $35,000 difference only compares cash received on day one. The two structures leave different balances, payments and sale obligations. Escrows may also have a remaining balance when the property sells. Reconcile proceeds with the commercial refinance worksheet and a payoff dated for the expected close.

Will the building cover both payments?

Add the annual debt service on both loans and compare it with the underwritten property cash flow. If the first requires $300,000 a year and the supplement would add $70,000, a hypothetical $480,000 of underwritten net cash flow produces about 1.30 times combined coverage. A lender also reviews total debt against property value and program limits.

Ask for prepayment schedules for both loans for your planned sale month. If one matures first, model the outstanding balance on the other. Share the first loan’s maturity, balance, approximate payment, requested additional funds and hold period for a first comparison.
