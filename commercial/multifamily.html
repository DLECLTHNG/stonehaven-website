Why It's Different

The property carries the file

Rent roll and trailing statement, not your tax return. NOI against target coverage sizes the loan; LTV is the ceiling. Our calculator runs that math.

The Sources

Agency, bank or bridge

Agency (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac) for stabilized: long fixed terms, non-recourse. Banks and credit unions for smaller loans. Bridge for value-add, with agency or bank as the exit.

Value-Add

Fund the plan, refinance the result

Bridge in, permanent out. We size the exit first: no takeout with margin, no bridge. See bridge vs. permanent.
