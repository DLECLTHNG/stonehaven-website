Which income can support the loan today?

On a stabilized acquisition, reconcile the seller’s trailing 12 months of operations with the latest rent roll. Adjust for a tax reassessment, a new insurance quote, management costs and concessions that continue after closing. On a refinance, add the dated payoff, any prepayment charge and the remaining term.

Fannie Mae’s conventional term sheet covers existing, stabilized properties with at least five units. Freddie Mac publishes separate conventional offerings, including fixed-rate, floating-rate and small loans. Compare the program that matches the property and loan size. Fannie Mae conventional eligibility · Freddie Mac conventional programs.

When do renovations change the answer?

A buyer plans to renovate ten apartments, two at a time. For each pair, put the move-out, permit, completion and first rent payment on a calendar. If the building’s remaining income carries the debt, a permanent loan with approved repair arrangements may be worth reviewing. If the loan depends on the rent increase and the owner needs construction draws, compare a bridge loan with a bank renovation facility.

Ask how much funds at closing, when renovation money can be drawn, what expenses the reserve covers and who pays for a delay. A larger commitment may provide less cash on acquisition day.

A change in rent can decide the exit

Illustrative refinance test. Assume the underwriter accepts $360,000 of annual net operating income and the proposal requires 1.25 times debt-service coverage. Dividing $360,000 by 1.25 leaves $288,000 for annual principal and interest. If recurring expenses rise by $40,000, the debt-service capacity drops to $256,000. A proposed $280,000 payment fits the first case and exceeds the second by $24,000 each year.

Use both cases before signing a bridge note. The commercial loan calculator lets you test how a different rate or amortization changes the principal that payment can support. The figures above illustrate the arithmetic and do not quote terms.

Compare the same closing and sale dates

Ask for a side-by-side showing cash required at closing, scheduled payments, reserves, repayment at sale and any exit or extension fee. Model a sale a year earlier than planned. An agency prepayment provision can affect early sale proceeds. A shorter bridge maturity can force a refinancing decision ahead of your preferred sale date.

Give Stonehaven your purchase price or payoff, requested financing, units offline and target closing date. We can organize the two cases and flag which assumptions need supporting documents before you commit to a structure.
