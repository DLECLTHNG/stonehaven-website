Straight Answers

Three things everyone asks

Will this reset the existing mortgage rate? No - a HELOC is a separate second lien; the current mortgage and its rate don't change.

Does checking affect credit? No - the estimate and the broker text conversation involve no credit pull.

How fast are funds available? Often a few weeks from a complete application - the broker gives a real timeline in the text conversation.

Do I need an appraisal for a HELOC? Valuation requirements vary by lender and scenario. Ask your advisor which method is required and what it costs before proceeding.

Should I choose a HELOC or a cash-out refinance? If your current mortgage rate is low, a second-lien HELOC or fixed home-equity loan usually protects it. If your current rate is high, replacing the loan with a cash-out refinance may make sense. The comparison depends on your rate, balance, and goal.
