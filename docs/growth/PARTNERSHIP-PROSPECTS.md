## First outreach batch

Start with Atlanta REIA, GaREIA, Atlanta NARPM, NAHREP Atlanta, and Premier Atlanta Real Estate. Ask for a short introductory conversation about audience needs and an educational resource. These are proposed talking points, not sent messages.

Offer: a 15-minute educational session, an original checklist, or an article that answers a recurring question. Ask about their audience, editorial process, existing financing relationships, and whether the proposed resource would be useful. A link is an editorial choice, not a condition of participating.

Track organization, contact, first contact date, reply, meeting, published resource URL, referral visits, qualified inquiries, applications, funded loans, and actual cost. Use a unique campaign link only after the organization agrees to share it. Example:

`https://stonehavencre.com/dscr?utm_source=atlanta_reia&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=partner_education&utm_content=dscr_checklist`

No referral fee, gift, or other thing of value in exchange for covered mortgage referrals. Genuine paid marketing requires appropriate review of the services and compensation. [CFPB RESPA FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act-faqs/).

Paid links should be qualified with sponsored or nofollow; do not buy ranking credit or require reciprocal links. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
