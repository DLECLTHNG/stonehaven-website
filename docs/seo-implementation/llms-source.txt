> Stonehaven Lending is a bilingual (English/Spanish) U.S. mortgage brokerage.
> It arranges home loans in Georgia, Alabama, Tennessee, Florida, North
> Carolina and South Carolina, and commercial real estate, SBA 7(a)/504 and
> DSCR rental-property financing nationwide. Stonehaven is not a direct lender;
> financing is subject to lender underwriting and program availability, which
> varies by state. Preliminary reviews and calculators are educational and are
> not approvals, commitments, rate quotes or guarantees.

Key facts:
- Type: mortgage brokerage (residential + commercial); founder-run

- Commercial, DSCR and SBA financing: nationwide, availability varies by state
