# Commercial asset-page expansion, September 18, 2026

Expanded the existing English and Spanish pages for multifamily, mixed-use and commercial property financing. This work owns only those six pages and this note.

## Content changes

- Multifamily: replaced the blanket no-tax-return and nonrecourse descriptions with property and sponsor requirements, agency/bank/bridge distinctions, cash-flow review, condition, reserves and contractual liability considerations.
- Mixed-use: removed the unsupported 80% residential-income qualification shortcut. Added income versus area, tenant concentration, lease rollover, re-leasing costs, reimbursements, permitted use and financing-fit explanations. The original hypothetical example is 2,000 / 10,000 square feet = 20% of area and $90,000 / $300,000 annual contract base rent = 30% of rent. These are expressly not program thresholds.
- Commercial property: removed unsupported claims that industrial and retail financing is readily available or that particular property types receive the best structures. Added property-specific diligence for industrial, retail, office, self-storage, hospitality and special-purpose assets, plus tenant rollover, owner-occupied business distinctions and actual guarantee requirements.
- Removed unsubstantiated public loan-range claims from body copy without changing the existing form's range choices.
- Linked the new construction, fix-and-flip and bridge product pages in the matching language. English-only guides and the commercial calculator are clearly labeled as English when linked from Spanish pages. Spanish asset pages no longer direct a CRE sizing request to the residential rental DSCR calculator.
- All six lead forms are byte-for-byte identical to the pre-change HEAD, including routing attributes, endpoints, fields, values, validation settings and consent. The introductory copy outside the forms now states text follow-up and describes the under-one-hour response as preliminary feedback, not a promise of final terms.

## Primary sources checked September 18, 2026

- [Freddie Mac borrower overview](https://mf.freddiemac.com/borrowers/get-started): multifamily properties with five or more units and the lender-network execution. No agency-approved status is claimed for Stonehaven.
- [Fannie Mae fixed-rate mortgage term sheet](https://multifamily.fanniemae.com/financing-options/fixed-rate-mortgage-loans-term-sheet): nonrecourse availability includes required exceptions. No offered loan amount, pricing or other program term is imported into these pages.
- [Fannie Mae multifamily property guidance](https://mfguide.fanniemae.com/node/3041): commercial-lease and qualifying-income considerations. The page states this is a specific program framework, not a universal mixed-use rule.
- [OCC Commercial Real Estate Lending handbook](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/commercial-real-estate-lending/pub-ch-commercial-real-estate.pdf): property-specific underwriting, income, sponsor, collateral and lease risks. These supervisory principles are not lender commitments.
- [SBA 504 overview](https://www.sba.gov/loans/504-loans/): operating-business eligibility and the exclusion of speculation or investment in rental real estate. No blanket SBA eligibility is promised.
