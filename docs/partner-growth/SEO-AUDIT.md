# Referral guide search and AI discovery audit

September 13, 2026. Scope: all four referral guides, English and Spanish.

Each guide retains a distinct audience and intent: residential real estate agents, rental property managers and investor agents, CPAs and attorneys, or mortgage brokers with DSCR/commercial scenarios. No search-volume or ranking claim is made without performance data.

Implemented direct opening answers with practical next steps; visible localized breadcrumbs and matching BreadcrumbList data; canonical-linked BlogPosting identities, author organization URL and publisher logo; article Open Graph type; synchronized source briefs; and links to all eight guides in the existing llms.txt directory. Corrected its outdated description of the blog. The publisher now emits article metadata and canonical-linked article identities for future posts too.

Verified unique titles/descriptions, one H1, reciprocal language alternates, sitemap inclusion, contextual internal links, adjacent citations, editorial/team links, and crawlable static text. robots.txt already allows search and AI crawlers. No article-level noindex or snippet restriction was found. Existing contact conversion paths remain in place.

Google's official guidance says foundational SEO applies to AI features, no special AI text file or schema is required, and crawling/indexing/serving are not guaranteed. llms.txt is maintained as a supplemental directory, not a ranking mechanism.

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Measurement still requires Search Console/Bing performance and actual indexing data. This release does not establish current rankings, indexation, or AI citations. Spanish copy has not received native human review.
