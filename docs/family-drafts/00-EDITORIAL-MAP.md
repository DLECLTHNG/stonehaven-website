## Consistency anchor

The live /family-housing-options page already carries reviewed language that the series must
not contradict. It states that under Fannie Mae's provision a parent or legal guardian may
receive owner-occupant treatment when purchasing housing for an adult child with a disability
who "cannot work or lacks enough income to qualify independently," that the lender confirms
eligibility and documentation, and that disability alone does not guarantee approval. It also
tells readers to keep medical and benefits information out of the inquiry form. Every post in
this series uses the same disjunctive phrasing and the same boundary.

## Voice and compliance rules applied

- No em dashes.
- No rates, APRs, down payment figures, or payment examples anywhere in the series, so no
  Regulation Z trigger terms are used and no additional advertising disclosures are required.
- Hypothetical examples are labeled and contain no numbers.
- No invented borrower stories, quotes, credentials, or results. Nothing is written from
  Chris De Leeuw's personal experience because none was supplied.
- Agency guidance, lender-dependent requirements, and general planning are visually separated
  in every post.
- The inquiry is described as a short conversation, never as an approval, prequalification,
  or application.
- Readers are never asked to send medical records, diagnoses, Social Security numbers, or
  benefits documents.
