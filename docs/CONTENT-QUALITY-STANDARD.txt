Stonehaven content quality standard
Adopted November 1, 2026

Purpose
Answer a financing decision clearly, then offer the next useful step. Apply to
new content and substantive revisions. Owner publication restrictions still
apply. This standard does not resume Family, FHA, VA, HELOC-persona or
first-time-buyer publication.

Search intent and metadata
Assign one primary question to each page. Use its language naturally in the
search title, description, opening answer and relevant headings. Check existing
pages before creating another URL. Do not add keyword repetition or location
variants without distinct information. Keep canonical, hreflang, social metadata
and the retained brief consistent.

Answer and explanation
Begin with a direct answer, including the qualification that materially changes
it. Define unfamiliar terms. Use H2 for decisions or stages and H3 for narrower
questions. Add a table when it makes a comparison easier. Use short paragraphs
and lists for real steps or parallel items. Do not force a word count. A long
construction guide should earn its length with different decisions, calculations,
document steps and failure cases, not repeated summaries.

Links
Place descriptive internal links where the reader encounters the related
question. A footer reading list is supplementary. Verify both route and fragment.
Use the corresponding Spanish page when it exists; label English-only resources
in Spanish copy. Replace generic source anchors with the publisher and topic.

Evidence and business identity
Use current primary sources near the claim they support. Prefer government,
regulator, agency and professional-standard sources over competing lenders.
Do not substitute agency conventional rules for a private DSCR program. Explain
whether a source governs banks, credit unions, consumer loans or a named program.
Do not remove a competitor citation while leaving an unsupported program claim.
Omit or qualify that claim unless another source supports it.

Stonehaven acts as mortgage broker. Link the genuine organizational byline to
management. Never invent a named expert review, credentials, borrower experience,
loan terms, reviews or closed deals. Label original calculations as hypothetical.
Keep company-reported closings separate, including their limits and land-equity
assumptions. Do not reinstate the removed aggregate funded-volume claim.

Questions and structured data
Write visible, specific questions with self-contained answers. Keep any schema
consistent with the visible page. FAQ content helps readers, but do not promise
FAQ rich results: Google retired that display feature in May 2026. Organization
and article metadata do not guarantee rankings or AI citations.

Video
Optional, not a ranking requirement. Add a video only if it answers a question
better than the text, comes from an appropriate publisher and has useful context
or an accessible text alternative. Prefer a user-initiated, privacy-conscious
load; do not add autoplay, tracking or blocking scripts just for time on page.
No video was added in the November 1 revision.

Inquiry and measurement safeguards
Keep name/email validation, $50,000 HELOC minimum and 640 reported-score minimum.
Preserve Netlify, office email and CRM routing and accepted-inquiry conversion
tracking. Public inquiries must not solicit private documents or identifiers.
Do not alter the secure application. Compare qualified leads and search visibility
using actual available reports; do not claim a ranking or conversion improvement
from publishing alone.

Revisions and validation
Use contentRevision in the retained brief only for an existing article receiving
a substantive revision. scripts/article_quality.py rebuilds that article inside
the existing page without changing its route or inquiry section. Keep the original
publication date. Update visible modification dates, BlogPosting and sitemap for
substantive changes only, not source-label corrections. Run check-site, all CI
generators, and relevant mobile/desktop browser checks. Confirm preview behavior
before publication and verify live URLs after an authorized production release.
