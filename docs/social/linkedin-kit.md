Stonehaven Lending is a real estate capital advisory and brokerage firm
serving investors, property owners, and business owners nationwide.

We arrange financing across three desks: commercial real estate
($1M–$50M+ structures for income-producing property), SBA 7(a) and 504
(business acquisition and owner-occupied real estate), and DSCR
rental-property loans for investors. Our founding team brings more than
two decades of mortgage operations, processing, and compliance
experience - which shows up as complete files, anticipated guidelines,
and honest answers about what a program will and won't do.

We are not a direct lender. Financing is arranged through third-party
capital providers and remains subject to lender underwriting and
program availability, which varies by state.

**Post 2 (thought-leadership, the binding constraint):**
Commercial loan proceeds aren't a negotiation. They're the output of
two constraints: what the property's income supports at a target DSCR,
and what its value supports at a maximum LTV. Whichever produces the
smaller loan is binding your deal - and knowing which one tells you
exactly what to negotiate.

DSCR-bound? Work the income: rents, expenses, amortization, rate.
LTV-bound? Work the value: the appraisal, the price, the equity.
Weeks get wasted pulling the wrong lever.

We built a calculator that names the binding constraint for your deal:
stonehavencre.com/commercial-loan-calculator
