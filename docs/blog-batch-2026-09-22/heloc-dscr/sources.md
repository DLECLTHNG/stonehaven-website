## Arithmetic and timing verification

- HELOC access: $100,000 limit less $35,000 drawn = $65,000 unused. Reduced $60,000 limit less $35,000 drawn = $25,000. Adding $8,000 allocated cash gives $33,000; a $40,000 remaining project leaves a $7,000 gap. A complete freeze leaves a $32,000 gap. Interest, fees and later changes excluded explicitly.
- Subordination: $360,000 / $600,000 = 60%; $380,000 / $600,000 = approximately 63.3%; $450,000 / $600,000 = 75%. Paying the $20,000 draw to zero does not change a retained $90,000 line limit. A common assumed value and no other liens are explicit.
- Closing: Monday opening plus all required notices/disclosures that day, with no intervening federal legal public holiday, leads to Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday as the three counted days. The example does not promise Friday funds. A later required delivery changes the calculation.
- DSCR cash: $100,000 + $12,000 + $4,500 + $14,400 + $4,000 = $134,900. Purchase consumption is $116,500; less $10,000 already paid as credited earnest money leaves $106,500 at closing. Retained reserves are not counted as a fee. Six assumed $2,400 payments = $14,400. $3,000 / $2,400 = 1.25. One $2,400 payment plus a $1,800 repair = $4,200; $14,400 less $4,200 = $10,200.
- Prepayment: Assumed $300,000 outstanding principal times 4% = $12,000; principal plus charge = $312,000 before other sums. Assumed 3% on unchanged principal = $9,000; difference $3,000. $250 x 24 months = $6,000 of payment cash flow, expressly not a complete economic savings calculation.

All examples are original hypotheticals, with no borrower identities, transaction claims, current rates, APRs, guaranteed approvals or promised closing dates.
