From an idea to a financeable project

  1. Define the outcome. Will you sell, hold for rent, or occupy part of the property? Describe the intended use accurately from the beginning.
  2. Build the budget. Separate the purchase, improvements, closing expenses, holding costs, contingency and cash reserves.
  3. Test the numbers. Look at a slower sale, lower rent, higher expenses or a smaller refinance. A deal should have a plan for more than its best outcome.
  4. Review financing fit. Match the lender to your experience, property condition, project size, location and exit plan.
  5. Prepare and compare. Use one consistent deal summary to compare written terms, cash requirements and conditions.

You can come to Stonehaven before you have a property under contract. Tell us what you are learning, the type of project you have in mind and the questions you want to answer. If figures are not known yet, say so.

How Stonehaven fits into your journey

Stonehaven Lending is a mortgage brokerage. We help you organize the scenario, review possible financing structures and seek placement through third-party lender relationships. A useful first conversation can identify a missing budget item, an unsuitable loan term or an exit assumption that needs more work.

We can help compare rental financing, acquisition and rehab funding, bridge loans and commercial options. Lenders make credit decisions and set conditions. Contractors, inspectors, attorneys, accountants and property managers have separate roles; financing support does not replace their work.

There is no need to make your first project sound bigger or more experienced than it is. An accurate picture gives us a better starting point.
