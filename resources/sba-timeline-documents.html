The SBA timeline and
document checklist

SBA paperwork is real. Almost every slow file is slow for preventable reasons.

By Christiaan De Leeuw · Stonehaven Lending · Updated July 30, 2026

The Short Answer

Pre-qualification, underwriting, approval, closing: several weeks to a few months. The biggest variable is how complete your file is.

The Stages

What happens, in order

Pre-qualification: program fit, sizing, eligibility.

Underwriting: financials, tax returns, property, guarantors.

Approval: terms issued; on a 504, the CDC works in parallel.

Closing: appraisal, environmental, title, insurance. Order early.

The Checklist

Documents to gather before you apply

  • iBusiness tax returns, three years, all schedules.
  • iiPersonal tax returns, owners of 20%+, three years.
  • iiiInterim financials, YTD P&L and balance sheet.
  • ivDebt schedule, every business obligation.
  • vPersonal financial statement for each guarantor.
  • viThe property, contract or LOI, plus construction plans and budgets.
  • viiEntity documents, formation papers, operating agreement, ownership.

Startups and expansions add a business plan with defensible numbers.

What Slows Files

The usual suspects

Unfiled tax returns. Ownership that doesn't match entity papers. Undocumented injection funds. Environmental findings. Late insurance. Fix them before underwriting.
