Questions

Before you run the numbers

Why does the 504 show two loans?

That's the program's structure: a bank finances roughly half the project, a Certified Development Company (CDC) funds a second portion backed by the SBA, and you inject the remainder. The two pieces carry different rates and terms, which is why the calculator prices them separately.

Is 10% down guaranteed?

No - 10% is the standard 504 injection for an established business buying a general-purpose building. Special-purpose properties and businesses under two years old each add 5%, and lender overlays or eligibility findings can change the picture. The calculator models the program's structure rules; a specialist confirms what applies to your project.

Which rate should I enter?

Your best current estimate - ask lenders you're talking to, or use a rate you consider realistic for your credit profile and market. We deliberately don't pre-fill market rates: they move, and a stale number would mislead you. The math is exact for whatever rate you enter.
