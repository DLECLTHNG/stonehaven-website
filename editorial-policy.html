Editorial standards

How to read our guides, examples, and calculators, and how to report a correction.

Who publishes this information

Stonehaven Lending publishes these resources as a mortgage brokerage, NMLS #1752355. Our management page identifies the team. An organization byline does not mean a particular person has reviewed every article. Individual authors or reviewers are identified only when that work has actually occurred.

Guides and completed transactions

Guides explain concepts and may use hypothetical examples. Those examples are not completed transactions. A closing write-up should be identified as such and omit or generalize client-identifying details. No example promises that another scenario will qualify or produce the same result.

Sources and dates

We link to sources used for specific factual claims where appropriate. Publication and update dates describe the content, not a live loan quote. Requirements can change. Before proceeding, confirm the lender terms applicable to your scenario with the broker.

Calculators and decisions

Calculators provide illustrations using the stated inputs and assumptions. See our calculation methodology. A result does not replace valuation, documentation, or lender underwriting. These pages do not provide legal or tax advice.

Availability

Residential service is described in Georgia, Alabama, Tennessee, Florida, North Carolina, and South Carolina. Business-purpose DSCR availability varies by state and program. Verify Stonehaven's registration through NMLS Consumer Access.

Report a correction

Email office@stonehavencre.com with the page address, the passage in question, and a relevant source. Do not include account numbers, Social Security numbers, or financial documents. For a personal scenario, use the contact form.
