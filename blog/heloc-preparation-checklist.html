HELOC preparation checklist: what to have ready

A printable checklist for a useful HELOC conversation, including your three starting figures, project timing, and questions about repayment.

Stonehaven Lending · 2026-09-12

Guide overview
PurposePrepare for a broker conversation
ScopeEducation, not a loan approval

You do not need a complete application to organize the first conversation. This checklist helps you distinguish the figures you know, the estimates you are using, and the questions you still need answered. Print this page or save it using your browser; nothing needs to be uploaded to use the checklist.

Your starting figures

Estimated home value: __________   Source and date of estimate: __________
Remaining mortgage balance: __________
Amount you want: __________
Other property liens to discuss: __________

Use zero if the mortgage is paid off. If you do not know an exact number, mark it as an estimate rather than presenting it as an appraisal or payoff quote. Keep the amount you want separate from the total equity you believe you have.

Your goal and timing

Purpose of funds: __________
When expenses are due: __________
One payment or several stages: __________
Payment budget to discuss: __________

For a project, bring the cost information you already have and identify items that are still uncertain. If a contractor date depends on financing, tell the broker that the date is a target. An inquiry does not reserve funds or commit a lender to a deadline.

Information to locate for later

Know where to find your latest mortgage statement, property information, insurance information, and records relevant to documenting your income. Ask the broker for the specific application checklist and secure delivery method if you decide to proceed. Requirements vary; this planning list does not claim that every lender will request the same documents.

Questions for the broker

What valuation method would this scenario need? What costs apply to opening, keeping, or closing the account? How would payments change? What happens when borrowing access ends? What assumptions still need confirmation? Write the answers next to the actual proposal instead of relying on a general advertisement.

A HELOC can have separate borrowing and repayment stages, with payment changes depending on the agreement. Read the CFPB explanation of HELOCs for those mechanics. For a product comparison, see HELOC versus cash-out refinancing.

Keep the public inquiry simple

Use only the requested contact and scenario fields. Do not send Social Security numbers, account numbers, identity documents, or statements through the public form or an unverified email address. The HELOC inquiry starts with home value, mortgage balance, and requested amount. A broker can explain the next step and the appropriate document channel.
