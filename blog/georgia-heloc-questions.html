Georgia HELOC questions to ask before requesting terms

A Georgia homeowner guide to checking the brokerage, organizing property details, and separating a HELOC inquiry from lender approval.

Stonehaven Lending · 2026-09-12

Guide overview
PurposePrepare for a broker conversation
ScopeEducation, not a loan approval

A useful Georgia HELOC conversation begins with your property and your goal. A city name alone does not establish program eligibility or a credit limit. Use these questions to organize a review with a mortgage broker and to identify which details still need confirmation.

Who is arranging the financing?

Stonehaven Lending is a mortgage brokerage based in Alpharetta, NMLS #1752355. Financing is arranged through third-party lenders. Confirm the business and licensing information using NMLS Consumer Access, and meet the people listed on our management page. A listing or a license does not itself approve a particular loan.

Which property figures should you prepare?

Bring your estimated home value, remaining mortgage balance, and requested amount. Also explain how the property is used and whether other loans are secured against it. If you have a current mortgage statement or a recent value estimate, identify its date. Do not treat an old listing price as an accepted lender valuation.

Ask which valuation process the proposed program requires and how its costs will be disclosed. A full appraisal, a different valuation method, and a borrower estimate are not interchangeable. Your scenario must be assessed under the actual lender requirements.

Where can Georgia homeowners find independent information?

The Georgia Department of Banking and Finance publishes home equity loan and line-of-credit information. It explains the distinction between product types and points consumers toward additional guidance. Use independent information alongside the actual lender disclosures, not as a substitute for them.

What should a quote comparison answer?

Ask what happens to your current mortgage, how funds become available, which costs apply, and how the payments could change. If you are comparing proposals, record the same requested amount and purpose for each. Different borrowing amounts or repayment periods can make an apparently simple comparison misleading.

If a line of credit is proposed, ask about access to future draws as well as the initial funds. A planned project should not depend on an assumption that every future draw will remain available regardless of changes. Ask the lender to explain the agreement and any conditions.

Does Stonehaven serve only Georgia homeowners?

Stonehaven describes residential service in Georgia, Alabama, Tennessee, Florida, North Carolina, and South Carolina, subject to program availability and applicable licensing. Property location and program fit still need review. Do not interpret the nationwide description of business-purpose DSCR financing as a nationwide residential HELOC offer.

What happens after the inquiry?

Use the preparation checklist and submit your three starting figures through the HELOC review form. Submitting is an inquiry, not a lender commitment. For the difference between an equity calculation and borrowing capacity, read how much equity could support a request.
