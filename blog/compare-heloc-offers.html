How to compare two HELOC offers

Compare access, rate structure, fees, and repayment terms with an ungated offer worksheet.

Stonehaven Lending · 2026-09-12

Guide overview
FocusA homeowner decision
ReviewLender requirements apply

Confirm that you are comparing the same product

The label on a marketing page is not a complete description of a loan. Ask whether the offer is an open line, a closed-end loan, or a line with particular advance and repayment rules. Compare the amount available for your actual purpose after any financed charges. If one offer provides a different amount, ask for comparable figures before choosing by payment.

Record the rate and the cash costs separately

Write down the note rate, APR, opening fees, ongoing charges, and potential early-closure costs. Also record which numbers are estimates and when an offer expires. A promotional rate needs an end date and an explanation of later pricing. Our offer worksheet intentionally leaves unknown terms blank; zero means something different from a term you have not confirmed.

Ask what happens after the first payment

Compare the initial advance, later borrowing access, repayment start, rate-change rules, and remaining balance at your expected payoff date. A fixed portion of a line may work differently from a fixed home equity loan. The useful question is how the offered contract fits your plan. Do not assume a product feature described by another lender is included in your offer.

Use the correct disclosures

The CFPB explains that HELOC borrowers do not receive the standard Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure used for many closed-end mortgages. Ask for the applicable home-equity disclosures and agreement. Bring questions to the broker without sending unredacted account documents through a public form. A comparison conversation is not a promise to beat another lender.

Prepare the next conversation

Use the home equity planning worksheet to organize your assumptions. Leave anything you do not know blank for discussion. The worksheet does not check eligibility or obtain a lender offer. You can request a broker conversation when you are ready to review your estimated home value, mortgage balance, and requested amount. Do not send account numbers or financial documents through a public inquiry form.
