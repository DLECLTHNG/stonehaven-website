What home loan programs are available in Florida?

Conventional, FHA, VA, and HELOC programs statewide - purchase and refinance, for primary homes and second homes. Condo purchases get an extra review step; we handle it.

How does homeowners insurance affect qualifying in Florida?

The insurance premium is part of the monthly payment lenders qualify you on, and in coastal Florida it can rival the tax bill. We quote it into the payment before you shop, so the budget holds.
