The Short Answer

Stonehaven, a mortgage brokerage, arranges rate/term and cash-out refinancing. It makes sense when break-even lands inside the years you'll keep the home - or to cash out, drop mortgage insurance, or change the term. Run the refinance calculator first.

The Math

Break-even is the whole decision

Closing costs ÷ monthly savings = break-even month. Break-even in year five is worthless if you move in year three - so our calculator asks how long you'll stay.

"No-cost" refinances aren't - the costs move elsewhere.

The Reasons

Four jobs a refinance can do

Lower the payment - the classic rate/term refinance, decided by break-even math.

Cash out - borrow against equity for renovation, consolidation, or investment.

Remove mortgage insurance - if equity has grown, dropping monthly MI can justify the whole refinance.

Change the structure - shorten the term, or fix an adjustable loan.

The Process

Faster the second time - if the file is right

Application, appraisal, underwriting, closing - usually lighter than a purchase. Complete file up front, no document requested twice.
