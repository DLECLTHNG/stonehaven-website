The Short Answer

Stonehaven Lending is a mortgage brokerage. We match you with a conventional, FHA, or VA program and manage the file to closing. Start with the payment calculator, then tell us about the home.

The Paths

Three ways into the same front door

Conventional - Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines. Mortgage insurance usually applies below 20% down.

FHA - government-insured. Down payments from 3.5% for qualifying credit and more flexible history, with mortgage insurance premiums.

VA - eligible veterans, service members, and certain surviving spouses. No down payment, no monthly mortgage insurance.

Investment property? DSCR loans qualify on rent, not your paycheck.
