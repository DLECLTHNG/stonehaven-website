Residential · VA

The VA loan - earned,
and worth using well.

For eligible veterans and service members, the VA loan is the strongest tool in residential finance. It deserves to be handled by people who respect both the benefit and the details. Residential programs are available in Georgia, Alabama, Tennessee, Florida, and North Carolina.

The Short Answer

A VA loan is a mortgage guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs for eligible veterans, active service members, and certain surviving spouses. Qualifying borrowers with full entitlement can buy a primary residence with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance - the two costs that weigh heaviest on other programs. There is a one-time VA funding fee (financeable, and waived for borrowers with qualifying service-connected disabilities). Eligibility runs through your Certificate of Eligibility, which we help obtain.

The Benefit

What makes VA genuinely different.

Zero down for qualifying borrowers with full entitlement, no monthly mortgage insurance, competitive pricing backed by the VA guaranty, and meaningful protections in underwriting - the VA's residual-income standard looks at whether the household actually has money left to live on, which is arguably the most borrower-protective test in lending. Entitlement can also be reused, and remaining entitlement can support another purchase in some situations - a detail worth checking rather than assuming, either way.

The Fine Print

The funding fee and the details, honestly.

The one-time funding fee varies with down payment and whether you've used the benefit before, and it's typically financed into the loan. Borrowers receiving compensation for service-connected disabilities - and certain surviving spouses - are exempt. The property must be your primary residence and meet VA minimum property requirements. None of this is fine print to hide; it's arithmetic we put in front of you before you commit.

The Process

COE to closing, managed.

The Certificate of Eligibility comes first - we help pull it early so it never blocks a contract date. From there the file runs like any well-managed mortgage: complete documentation up front, the VA appraisal ordered promptly, and honest communication if anything in the property or the file needs attention. Two decades of mortgage operations means the benefit you earned gets processed like it matters.
