Deposits need context

A bank statement mortgage still requires documented income and lender underwriting. Transfers between accounts, borrowed funds and other non-income deposits may need to be excluded. Business expenses, ownership share and income trends can affect the result. Total deposits are not automatically your qualifying income.

Questions before you start

Does this mean no income verification?

No. Bank statements provide a way to document income. The lender still verifies the file and applies its qualification requirements.

Will I need 12 or 24 months of statements?

That depends on the program and your circumstances. Ask which consecutive statements are needed and whether personal or business accounts are appropriate before collecting documents.

Can I use this to buy a rental?

Property use affects program eligibility. For an investment property, compare a bank statement review with a DSCR review based on the property’s eligible rental income.

What credit score or down payment is required?

Requirements vary with the lender, loan amount, property and overall file. We review those details before presenting an available option.

Should I upload my statements here?

No. This is an initial inquiry. Keep account numbers, statements, tax returns and other private documents out of the public form. A specialist can arrange a secure next step.
