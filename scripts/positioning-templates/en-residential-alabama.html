Confirm the homestead treatment for your own purchase

Alabama’s Department of Revenue describes homestead eligibility around ownership and primary occupancy. Do not assume the seller’s exemption or tax bill will apply to you. Confirm the parcel’s assessment and your eligibility with the county office before finalizing the payment budget.

Questions about your purchase

Can I discuss a purchase before I am under contract?

Yes. Share the price range, county, down payment and income type. The conversation identifies what needs review; it is not underwriting approval or a closing guarantee.

Can you review financing for a second home?

Yes. Explain the intended personal use and any rental plans accurately. The financing structure and eligibility depend on the property and program.

Do I need W-2 income?

Documentation depends on how you earn. We can review salary or self-employment scenarios, but not every income source or deposit qualifies.

From asking price to a mortgage plan

Whether you are considering Birmingham, Huntsville or the Gulf Coast, start with the amount you intend to borrow. Compare the down payment with reserves and closing uses. Our jumbo mortgage guide explains why not every high-value home requires a jumbo loan.

For business owners, the self-employed mortgage guide separates revenue, profit and personal funds. Where appropriate, we can review a bank-statement mortgage. This is a documentation path to evaluate, not an approval or a no-documentation loan.

Share the state or city, price range, desired loan, intended use and timeline. Keep private financial documents out of the public inquiry form.
