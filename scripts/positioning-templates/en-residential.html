Conforming or jumbo?

Home value and loan amount are different figures. Depending on the down payment, a $1 million home may use a conforming or jumbo mortgage. The 2026 FHFA baseline conforming limit for a one-unit home is $832,750; some counties have higher limits. The loan must also meet the lender’s other requirements.
