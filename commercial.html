Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a commercial real estate loan?

Debt secured by income property, underwritten on its cash flow and value - not your salary.

How much down payment do I need for a commercial property?

Set as loan-to-value, by property and borrower. Owner-occupied? SBA financing can go to 10% down.

What are typical commercial loan terms?

$1M to $50M+, up to 30 years, fixed or floating. Request a term sheet for your property.

What property types do you finance?

Multifamily, industrial, retail, office and mixed-use - stabilized, value-add, construction and bridge.

How long does a commercial real estate loan take to close?

Varies by asset. Send statements, rent roll and contract up front and we hold the term-sheet timeline, subject to appraisal and title.

Do you arrange financing nationwide?

Yes - anywhere in the U.S.
